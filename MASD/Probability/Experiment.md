# Random experiments

A phenomenon whose outcome cannot be predetermined or predicted with certainty like
- Roll of a die
- Roll a die three times
- Flip a coin

**Outcome** is the result of the experiment.
*Element*
- Roll a die --> 3
- Roll a die three times --> (2, 4, 6)

**Event** is a collection of possible outcomes.
*Set*
- Roll a die (event=$E$)
$$
\begin{matrix}
E_{1}=\set{1, 3, 5} & E_{2}=\set{2,5} & =\set{6}
\end{matrix}
$$
**Sample space** is the set of all possible outcomes.
*Universal set*
- Rolle a die
$$
\Omega = \set{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}
$$
- Roll a die three times
$$
\Omega=\set{(1, 1, 1), (1, 1, 2),\dots ,(5,6,6),(6,6,6)}
$$
> We say that an event occurs if the outcome of the experiment is an element of $A$
