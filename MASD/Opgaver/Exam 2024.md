$$
### a)
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left( \frac{x^2-y^2}{xy} \right) & = 
\frac{(x^2-y^2)'(xy)-(x^2-y^2)(xy)'}{(xy)^2} \\
 & = \frac{2x(xy)-y(x^2-y^2)}{(xy)^2} \\
 & = \frac{y^3+yx^2}{x^2y^2} \\
 & = \frac{y^2+x^2}{x^2y}
\end{align}
$$

### b)
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left( \frac{x^2-y^2}{xy} \right) & = 
\frac{(x^2-y^2)'(xy)-(x^2-y^2)(xy)'}{(xy)^2} \\
 & = \frac{xy^2-2xy^2-x^3}{y^2x^2} \\
 & = \frac{-y^2-x^2}{xy^2} \\
 & = -\frac{y^2+x^2}{xy^2}
\end{align}
$$

 \\

- If the Hessian matrix is indefinite (the Hessian matrix has positive and negative eigenvalues), the critical point is a **saddle point**.
