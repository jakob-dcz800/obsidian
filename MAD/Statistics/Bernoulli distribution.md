Given a random variable $X$ with two possible outcomes, $0$ or $1$, where the probability that it takes the value 1 is $q ∈ (0,1)$. Then, the Bernoulli distribution is given by
$$
P(X=x) = q^x(1-q)^{1-x}
$$
Example:
$$
\begin{align}
P(X=1) & =q \\
P(X=0) & =1-q
\end{align}
$$
We write $X \sim \text{Bernoulli}(q)$
