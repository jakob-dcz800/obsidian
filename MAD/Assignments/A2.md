## 1 a

$$
\mathcal{L}
= \frac{1}{N} \sum^{N}_{n=1} \alpha_{n}(f(\mathbf{x}_{n}; \mathbf{w})-t_{n})^2
= \frac{1}{N} \sum^{N}_{n=1}\alpha_{n}(\mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{x}_{n}-t_{n})^2
$$
Ethvert $\alpha_{n}$ er en unik konstant værdi.
$\alpha_{n}$ er diagonalværdien en en diagonalmatrix $\mathbf{A}$:

$$
\mathbf{A}=
\begin{bmatrix}
\alpha_{1} & 0 & \dots  &  0 \\
0 & \alpha_{2} & \dots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \alpha_{n}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
show by mathematical derivation that the optimal least squares parameter value is 
$$
\hat{\mathbf{w}} = (\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{AX})^{−1}\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{At}
$$

---
$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}(\mathbf{w}) & =
\frac{1}{N} \sum^{N}_{n=1}\alpha_{n}(\mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{x}_{n}-t_{n})^2  \\
 & = \frac{\mathbf{A}}{N} (\mathbf{w} \mathbf{X} - \mathbf{t})^T (\mathbf{w}\mathbf{X}-\mathbf{t}) \\
& =\frac{\mathbf{A}}{N} ((\mathbf{w} \mathbf{X})^T-\mathbf{t}^T)(\mathbf{w}\mathbf{X}-\mathbf{t}) \\
 & = \frac{A}{N} \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{wX} - \frac{A}{N}\mathbf{t}^T \mathbf{wX} - \frac{A}{N} (\mathbf{wX})^T \mathbf{t} + \frac{A}{N} \mathbf{t}^T \mathbf{t} \\
 & = \frac{A}{N} \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{wX} - \frac{2A}{N} \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{t} + \frac{A}{N} \mathbf{t}^T \mathbf{t} \\
 & = \frac{A}{N} \left( \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{wX} - 2\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{t} + \mathbf{t}^T \mathbf{t} \right)
\end{align}
$$

Then we calculate $\nabla \mathcal{L}$ I.e. $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{w}}$

$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{w}}\left( 
\frac{A}{N} \left( \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{wX} - 2\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{t} + \mathbf{t}^T \mathbf{t} \right) \right) & =
\frac{A}{N} \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{w}}
\left( \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{wX} - 2\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{t} + \mathbf{t}^T \mathbf{t} \right) \\
& =
\frac{A}{N}
\left(
\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{wX}
- 2\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{t}
\right)
\end{align}
$$
## 1.a - again


The weighted loss is:
$$
\mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^N \alpha_n (\mathbf{w}^\top \mathbf{x}_n - t_n)^2  
$$
Then the loss can be written as:
$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{L} & = \frac{1}{N} (\mathbf{X}\mathbf{w} - \mathbf{t})^\top \mathbf{A} (\mathbf{X}\mathbf{w} - \mathbf{t})  
\end{align}
$$
We find the gradient:
$$
\begin{align}
\nabla_{\mathbf{w}} \mathcal{L} & = \frac{\partial }{\partial \mathbf{w}}
 \left( \frac{1}{N} (\mathbf{X}\mathbf{w} - \mathbf{t})^T \mathbf{A} (\mathbf{X}\mathbf{w} - \mathbf{t})  \right)  \\
 & = \frac{1}{N} \frac{\partial}{\partial \mathbf{w}} (\mathbf{u}^T\mathbf{Au}) \\
\end{align}
$$
We use chain rule $\frac{\partial \mathbf{u}}{\partial \mathbf{w}}=X$ and get
$$
\nabla_{\mathbf{w}} \mathcal{L} = \frac{2}{N} \mathbf{X}^\top \mathbf{A} (\mathbf{X}\mathbf{w} - \mathbf{t})
$$

For the optimal $\hat{\mathbf{w}}$ set $\nabla_{\mathbf{w}} \mathcal{L} = 0$  

$$
\begin{align}
\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{A} (\mathbf{X} \hat{\mathbf{w}} - \mathbf{t})  & = 0 \\
\implies  
\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{A} \mathbf{X}
\hat{\mathbf{w}} &  = \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{A} \mathbf{t} \\
\implies
\hat{\mathbf{w}} & = (\mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{A} \mathbf{X})^{-1} \mathbf{X}^T \mathbf{A} \mathbf{t}  
\end{align}
$$

## 3. a

PDF is given by derivative of CDF:

$$
PDF=
\begin{cases}
0 & x\leq 0 \\
\frac{d}{dx} (1-\exp(-\beta^{x\alpha}))  & x>0
\end{cases}
$$
Thus we calculate:
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{d}{dx} (1-\exp(-\beta^{x\alpha}))
\end{align}
$$
We use the chain rule:
$$
\begin{align}
(e^{g(x)})'=g(x)'e^{g(x)}
\end{align}
$$
$$
g(x)'=(-\beta x^\alpha)')=\beta\cdot \alpha x^{\alpha-1}
$$
Thus we get the PDF:
$$
PDF =
\begin{cases}
0 & x \leq 0 \\
\beta\cdot \alpha x^{\alpha-1}e^{-\beta x^\alpha} & x > 0
\end{cases}
$$

\begin{align}
P(X > 4) & = 1-F(4)  \\

 & \simeq 0.00193
\end{align}
$$
