
## 1.a)

$$
f(x, y) = x^4y^3+7x^5-e^{xy}
$$

$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} & = 
\frac{\partial }{\partial x}( x^4y^3+7x^5-e^{xy}) \\
 & =4x^3y^3+35x^4-ye^{xy}
\end{align}
$$

$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} & = 
\frac{\partial }{\partial y}( x^4y^3+7x^5-e^{xy}) \\
 & = 3x^4y^2-xe^{xy}
\end{align}
$$

## 1.b)

$$
f(x,y)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^3+xy+y^2}} = (x^3+xy+y^2)^{-1/2}
$$
We use the chain rule to say that $g=x^3+xy+y^2$
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} & = -\frac{1}{2}g^{-2/3} \\
\frac{\partial g}{\partial x} & = 3x^2+y
\end{align}
$$
$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}
= -\frac{1}{2}(x^3+xy+y^2) (3x^2+y)
$$
for y: $\frac{\partial g}{\partial y} = x+2y$
Then we get:
$$
-\frac{1}{2}(x^3+xy+y^2)^{-2/3}(x+2y)
$$
## 1.c)

We have
$$
f(x,y)=\frac{x^3+y^2}{x+y}
$$
We use the quotient rule
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}
& = \frac{(3x^2)(x+y)-(x^3+y^2)}{(x+y)^2}  \\
& = \frac{3x^3+3x^2y-x^3-y^2}{(x+y)^2}  \\
& = \frac{2x^3+3x^2y-y^2}{(x+y)^2}
\end{align}
$$

and for $\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}$:
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}
 & =\frac{2y(x+y)-x^3-y^2}{(x+y)^2} \\
 & =\frac{2xy+2y^2-x^3-y^2}{(x+y)^2} \\
 & =\frac{2xy+y^2-x^3}{(x+y)^2} \\
\end{align}
$$
