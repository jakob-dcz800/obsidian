- Write up the variance as an equation.
![[Pasted image 20251202195707.png]]

We know that
$$
\begin{align}
\mu & = E[X] \\
\sigma^2 & = E[(X-\mu)^2] \\
\implies (\sigma ^2)^2 & = (E[(X-\mu)^2])^2
\end{align}
$$
Jensens inequality states that for convex functions
$$
E[g(X)] \geq g(E[X])
$$
We saw before that we can substitute the convex function $g(X)=u^2$ - thus:
$$
\begin{align}
(E[(X-\mu)^2])^2 & \leq E[((X-\mu)^2)^2]  \\
\implies \sigma^4 & \leq E[(X-\mu)^4]
\end{align}
$$
# 3

Ask the question - do we have a known or unknown variance?

![[Pasted image 20251207181528.png]]

We already know the variance.
$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{N} (0,1) & \sim \sqrt{ n } \frac{\hat{\mu}-\mu}{\hat{\sigma}} \\
 & = \sqrt{ n } \frac{\hat{\mu}-\mu}{\sqrt{ \frac{1}{n-1} \sum ^n _{i=1}(X_{i}-\hat{\mu})^2}}
\end{align}
$$
We choose a confidence level of $95 \%$.
We find c from the table:
$$
c=1.69
$$
We have the mean of the sample = $\overline{x}= \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i}$
We then compute 
$$
\begin{align}
k & = \frac{c\sigma}{\sqrt{ n }} \\
 & = \frac{c \sqrt{ \frac{1}{n-1} \sum ^n _{i=1}(X_{i}-\hat{\mu})^2}}{\sqrt{ n }} \\
 & = 1.69 \cdot \sqrt{ \frac{ \frac{1}{n-1} \sum ^n _{i=1}(X_{i}-\hat{\mu})^2}{n} }
\end{align}
$$
And get the confidence interval:
$$
\text{CONF }_{\gamma} \set{\overline{x} - k \leq \mu \leq \overline{x} + k}
$$

Step 1: 
Data is modelled. We assume that the differences in days are normally distributed.
$X-Y = \{ 1.0, 0.5, -0.5, 1.5, 0.5 \}$

Step 2: 
H0: $μ \leq \frac{1}{n}(X-Y) = 0$ (no change)
HA: $\mu \leq \frac{1}{n}(X-Y) \not = 0$

Step 3:
Where the mean of differences are: $\overline D$ and $S$​ is the sample standard deviation of the differences
$$
T= \frac{\overline D - \mu_{0}}{S/\sqrt{ n }}
$$

Step 4:
The significance level is $\alpha = 0.05$

Step 5:
We use a two sided test and look up the rejection region in python using this value:
$$
\frac{1+\gamma}{2} = \frac{1+0.95}{2}=0.975
$$
And the code:

print("rejection region: ", scipy.stats.t.ppf((1 + 0.95) / 2, df=4))

We get $\sim 2.77$

Step 6:
$$
\begin{align}
T & = \frac{\overline D - \mu_{0}}{S/\sqrt{ n }} \\
 & = \frac{0.6-0}{S\sqrt{ 5 }} \\
 & = \frac{0.6-0}{0.74 / \sqrt{ 5 }} \\

$$
