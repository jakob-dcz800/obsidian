## 4.1 Write RC queries

1. What are the titles of board games for 2 to 4 players designed by Bruno Cathala

```fsharp
EXISTS gid, year.
Game(gid, title, "Bruno Cathala", 2, 4, year)
```

2. Which members (their names) own a copy of the games titled “Ticket to Ride”?
```fsharp
EXISTS mid, gid, pw, email, d, minp, maxp.
Owns(mid, gid)
AND Game(gid, "Ticket to Ride", d, minp, maxp)
AND Member(mid, name, pw, email)
```

3. Which members (their names) have rated all the games they own?
```fsharp
EXISTS mid, gid, pw, email, rating.

Owns(mid, gid)
AND Member(mid, name, pw, email)
AND (
	FORALL Rates(mid, gid, rating)
)
```

![[Pasted image 20260618115550.png]]

```fsharp
EXISTS mid, pw, email.
(FORALL gid. 
	Owns(mid, gid) IMPLIES
	(EXISTS r.
	Rates(mid, gid, r))
)
AND Member(mid, name, pw, email) // prop password
```

Alternatively:
there does not exist a game that the member owns and has not rated.
```fsharp
EXISTS mid, pw, email.

Member(mid, name, pw, email)
AND NOT EXISTS gid.
    Owns(mid, gid)
AND NOT EXISTS r.
    Rates(mid, gid, r)
```
