#### b)
> Can string `0111` be derived from $G$? 
> If it can, show a rightmost derivation $S ⇒$ ..

Yes;
$$
\begin{align}
S & \implies X Y \\
 & \implies X 1 Y \\
 & \implies X 1 1Y \\
 & \implies X 111 \\
 & \implies 0111
\end{align}
$$
