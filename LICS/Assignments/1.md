(e) Cancer will not be cured unless its cause is determined and a new drug for cancer is found.

| atom | sentence                          |
| ---- | --------------------------------- |
| p    | Cancer will be cured              |
| q    | The cause of cancer is determined |
| r    | A new drug for cancer is found    |
If the cause is determined and a new drug for cancer is found, then cancer will  be cured
$$
q \land r \to p
$$
If the cause is undetermined and a new drug for cancer isn't found, then cancer will not be cured
$$
\neg q \lor \neg  r
\to \neg p
$$

| atom | sentence           |
| ---- | ------------------ |
| p    | It will rain       |
| q    | The sun will shine |
$$
p \lor q \to \neg (p \land q)
$$
