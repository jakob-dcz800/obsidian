## Operand

The object of a mathematical operation, i.e., it is the object or quantity that is operated on.

## Units
[[CompSys/Hardware/Memory hierarchy|Memory]] is byte-addressed, so it is a good idea to convert everything to bytes.
- Kibibyte (KiB): 1024 bytes
- Mebibyte (MiB) = 1024 KiB 
- Kilobit = 125 bytes
