## Definition

$$
A^T = 
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} & \cdots & a_{1n}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
a_{m1} & \cdots & a_{mn}
\end{bmatrix}^T
=
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} & \cdots & a_{m1}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
a_{1n} & \cdots & a_{mn}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

En $n × n$ matrix $A$ er [[Symmetriske matricer|symmetrisk]] hvis $A^T = A$
## Regneregler
$$
(A+B)^T=A^T+B^T
$$
$$
(A^T)^T=A
$$
$$
(AB)^T=B^TA^T
$$
